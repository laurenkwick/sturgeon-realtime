Atlantic Sturgeon Tracker

Guide

This interactive mapping application uses real data collected in the James River of Virginia. It is updated weekly and highlights the movement of Atlantic Sturgeon (Acipenser oxyrinchus) for the previous week. This guide demonstrates how to use and interpret the map.

The yellow circles displayed along the river correspond to receivers which detect sturgeon as they swim by. Larger circles reflect receivers that detected more sturgeon in a given week. Hovering over the circles will display a pop-up containing the Receiver Name and number of sturgeon detected that week.

Click a yellow circle to see the individual sturgeon that were detected at your selected receiver. Individual sturgeon are color coded by their sex in red for female, blue for male, and gray for unknown. Hover over these circles to view each sturgeon's name.

Clicking on an individual sturgeon will update the information panel with facts about the selected sturgeon including name, sex, capture date, capture location, spawning season, and fork length. An image of the selected sturgeon will also appear. Alternatively, you can choose from a list of sturgeon detected for the given week by using the dropdown menu instead of the map interface.

Once you have selected a sturgeon, you can click on the yellow timelapse button to play an animation of that sturgeon's movement during the week. A red circle will animate at each receiver that detected the selected sturgeon as the time label increments through each day.

Now it's your turn to explore!
